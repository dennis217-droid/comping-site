Comping

See what similar items just sold for on eBay — on any product page.

Comping is a browser extension for resellers doing online arbitrage. It shows eBay sold prices and an estimated profit margin for whatever you're looking at, so you can tell in seconds whether an item is worth flipping.

See pricing Privacy policy

How it works

Automatic detection

Comping recognizes product pages automatically as you browse, including Amazon — no need to search eBay yourself.

One click for the numbers

Click the small badge that appears to see the eBay sold-price median, range, and a profit estimate compared to the page's price — after eBay's fees and shipping.

Works everywhere else too

No product data on the page? Highlight any text, right-click, and choose "Check eBay sold prices" — or use the toolbar search box.

What makes it different

Currency-aware

Automatically compares against the right regional eBay marketplace, so the profit math never mixes currencies.

Only shows up when it should

Stays out of the way on search results, carts, and checkout pages — only shows up on real product pages. Per-site opt-out if you don't want it on a given store.

No account, no server

Everything Comping stores lives only in your own browser. Read the full privacy policy.

Accurate comps, not just "similar items"

Filtered before the median is calculated

A plain eBay search mixes in accessories, damaged or "for parts" listings, counterfeits, multi-item lots, and different variants of the same product. Comping filters all of that out first, based on a systematic audit of 1,000 real products. Read how the accuracy work was done.
